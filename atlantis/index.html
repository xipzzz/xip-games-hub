Warm Nordic
Off-white 3-seat sofa + wood-frame lounge chair, keeping the balcony opening clear. An oval coffee table and flat-woven rug frame the seating area; olive cushions, a warm floor lamp and plants add layers.

Design sizes: sofa 235 × 95 cm; coffee table 115 × 64 cm; rug 300 × 200 cm.

Wall colour, tiles, TV area and dining table stay as they are. The view stays the same when switching, for easy comparison.
